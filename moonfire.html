Abstract

Fires pose significant dangers, disrupting lives and livelihoods. They are disasters triggered by natural, artificial, or human-made factors. To ensure personal safety, early warnings are crucial. A CCTV system with fire, smoke, and gas sensors provides real-time monitoring and safeguards homes, workplaces, and commercial areas against potential threats. The study proposed an Arduino Uno prototype project called MONIFIRE, which integrates cameras with fire alarm systems to enhance security in buildings and construction sites. The system, which was assessed based on ISO 25010 standards and the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), provides timely information to homeowners and firefighters, helping reduce losses and potential fatalities. Future improvements may include additional features and alignment with updated ISO standards. The Arduino Uno prototype project, MONIFIRE, is a fire monitoring system with a CCTV camera and alert notification feature. It was assessed by IT professionals, students, and personnel from the Guimba Fire Station. The system was evaluated through survey involving 100 respondents consisting of 50 IT students, 30 other students, 10 professionals, and 10 fire station personnel. The system’s functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, usability, reliability, security, maintainability, and portability were evaluated based on ISO 25010 and the Technology Acceptance Model.
